“It’s déjà vu all over again” aptly describes each news headline about the next major data breach. Throughout history, if someone had valuable information, others would seek access to that information. This hide-and-seek dynamic becomes especially critical in the crucible of war, where a data breach can mean lost lives or even defeat.
